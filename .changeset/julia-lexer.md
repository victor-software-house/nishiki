Highlight Julia with a lexer of its own, on by default and in `all`.
The `lang-julia` feature leaves the opt-in `textmate` tier.
It turns on Python, JavaScript and SQL, which Julia's `py"""`, `js"""` and `sql"""` string macros embed.
Markdown's Julia fences are now highlighted as Julia in a default build, where they were plain text before.
