Highlight Perl with nishiki's TextMate engine on Shiki's `perl` grammar, behind the `lang-perl` feature in the opt-in `textmate` tier.
Its heredocs hand HTML, XML, CSS, JavaScript and SQL to their lexers, and a Markdown fence named `perl`, `pl`, `pm`, `pod`, `t`, `psgi` or `vcl` embeds it.
