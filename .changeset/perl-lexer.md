Highlight Perl with a lexer of its own, on by default and in `all`.
The `lang-perl` feature leaves the opt-in `textmate` tier.
It turns on CSS, HTML, JavaScript, SQL and XML, which Perl's heredocs embed.
Markdown's `perl` fences are now highlighted as Perl in a default build, where they were plain text before.
