L ### Why is the dependency resolution process slow?
T 3 text.html.markdown markup.heading.markdown heading.3.markdown punctuation.definition.heading.markdown
T 4 text.html.markdown markup.heading.markdown heading.3.markdown
T 50 text.html.markdown markup.heading.markdown heading.3.markdown entity.name.section.markdown
L 
L While the dependency resolver at the heart of Poetry is highly optimized and
T 76 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L should be fast enough for most cases, with certain sets of dependencies,
T 72 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L it can take time to find a valid solution.
T 42 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L 
L This is due to the fact that not all libraries on PyPI have properly declared their metadata
T 92 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L and, as such, they are not available via the PyPI JSON API. At this point, Poetry has no choice
T 95 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L but to download the packages and inspect them to get the necessary information. This is an expensive
T 100 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L operation, both in bandwidth and time, which is why it seems this is a long process.
T 84 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L 
L At the moment, there is no way around it. However, if you notice that Poetry
T 76 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L is downloading many versions of a single package, you can lessen the workload
T 77 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L by constraining that one package in your pyproject.toml more narrowly. That way,
T 80 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L Poetry does not have to sift through so many versions of it, which may speed up
T 79 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L the locking process considerably in some cases.
T 47 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L 
L {{% note %}}
T 12 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L Once Poetry has cached the releases' information on your machine, the dependency resolution process
T 99 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L will be much faster.
T 20 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L {{% /note %}}
