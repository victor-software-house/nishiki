L ### pycodestyle
T 15 text.html.markdown
L 
L [pycodestyle](https://pycodestyle.pycqa.org/en/stable/) is a code linter. It warns you
T 86 text.html.markdown
L of syntax errors, possible bugs, stylistic errors, etc. For the most part, pycodestyle
T 86 text.html.markdown
L follows [PEP 8](https://peps.python.org/pep-0008/) when warning about stylistic errors.
T 87 text.html.markdown
L There are a few deviations that cause incompatibilities with _Black_.
T 69 text.html.markdown
L 
L #### Configuration
T 18 text.html.markdown
L 
L ```ini
T 6 text.html.markdown
L max-line-length = 88
T 20 text.html.markdown
L ignore = E203,E701
T 18 text.html.markdown
L ```
T 3 text.html.markdown
L 
L (labels/why-pycodestyle-warnings)=
T 34 text.html.markdown
L 
L #### Why those options above?
T 29 text.html.markdown
L 
L ##### `max-line-length`
T 23 text.html.markdown
L 
L As with isort, pycodestyle should be configured to allow lines up to the length limit of
T 88 text.html.markdown
L `88`, _Black_'s default.
T 24 text.html.markdown
L 
L ##### `E203`
T 12 text.html.markdown
L 
L In some cases, as determined by PEP 8, _Black_ will enforce an equal amount of
T 78 text.html.markdown
L whitespace around slice operators. Due to this, pycodestyle will raise
T 70 text.html.markdown
L `E203 whitespace before ':'` warnings. Since this warning is not PEP 8 compliant, it
T 84 text.html.markdown
L should be disabled.
T 19 text.html.markdown
L 
L ##### `E701` / `E704`
T 21 text.html.markdown
L 
L _Black_ will collapse implementations of classes and functions consisting solely of `..`
T 88 text.html.markdown
L to a single line. This matches how such examples are formatted in PEP 8. It remains true
T 88 text.html.markdown
L that in all other cases Black will prevent multiple statements on the same line, in
T 83 text.html.markdown
L accordance with PEP 8 generally discouraging this.
T 50 text.html.markdown
L 
L However, `pycodestyle` does not mirror this logic and may raise
T 63 text.html.markdown
L `E701 multiple statements on one line (colon)` in this situation. Its
T 69 text.html.markdown
L disabled-by-default `E704 multiple statements on one line (def)` rule may also raise
T 84 text.html.markdown
L warnings and should not be enabled.
T 35 text.html.markdown
L 
L ##### `W503`
T 12 text.html.markdown
L 
L When breaking a line, _Black_ will break it before a binary operator. This is compliant
T 87 text.html.markdown
L with PEP 8 as of
T 16 text.html.markdown
L [April 2016](https://github.com/python/peps/commit/c59c4376ad233a62ca4b3a6060c81368bd21e85b#diff-64ec08cc46db7540f18f2af46037f599).
T 131 text.html.markdown
L There's a disabled-by-default warning in Flake8 which goes against this PEP 8
T 77 text.html.markdown
L recommendation called `W503 line break before binary operator`. It should not be enabled
T 88 text.html.markdown
L in your configuration. You can use its counterpart
T 50 text.html.markdown
L `W504 line break after binary operator` instead.
T 48 text.html.markdown
L
