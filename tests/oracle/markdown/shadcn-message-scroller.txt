L ## What Makes a Great Streaming Chat Experience
T 47 text.html.markdown
L 
L Building a chat interface used to be simple. You create an inverted list with
T 77 text.html.markdown
L an input. Type a message, it appends at the bottom. When a reply comes in, the
T 78 text.html.markdown
L list grows and scrolls. Done.
T 29 text.html.markdown
L 
L Streaming breaks that model. Messages arrive in chunks while you may still be
T 77 text.html.markdown
L reading, scrolling, or looking somewhere else entirely.
T 55 text.html.markdown
L 
L Now the challenge is preserving the reader's place while the conversation keeps
T 79 text.html.markdown
L changing. Get that wrong and the experience feels jumpy: people are pulled to
T 77 text.html.markdown
L the bottom, lose context, and have to find their way back.
T 58 text.html.markdown
L 
L In practice, this comes down to scroll: when to follow, when to hold, and when
T 78 text.html.markdown
L to let the reader decide. A great streaming chat should:
T 56 text.html.markdown
L 
L 1. **Move only when the reader asked to move.** If someone is reading, don’t pull them somewhere else. Auto-scroll should never be the default.
T 145 text.html.markdown
L 2. **Follow only while they’re following.** If they’re at the live edge, keep the stream in view. If they scroll away, leave them there.
T 140 text.html.markdown
L 3. **Every interaction is a signal.** Scrolling is not the only one. Selecting text, using the keyboard, opening a link, or searching should all stop the interface from moving.
T 176 text.html.markdown
L 4. **Start a new turn near the top of the viewport.** This gives the new turn somewhere it can be read from the beginning.
T 122 text.html.markdown
L 5. **Then stream in the answer.** The answer should grow into the screen, not immediately push everything away.
T 111 text.html.markdown
L 6. **Keep part of the previous conversation in context.** The prompt and reply should stay visually connected, and enough of the previous turn should remain visible so the reader knows where they are.
T 200 text.html.markdown
L 7. **Let new content arrive offscreen.** The conversation can keep streaming without changing what the reader is looking at.
T 124 text.html.markdown
L 8. **Show what’s happening out of view.** Make it clear when a response is still streaming or when new messages have arrived.
T 127 text.html.markdown
L 9. **Make it easy to return to the latest reply.** A “Jump to latest” action should bring the reader back and resume following.
T 131 text.html.markdown
L 10. **Let people jump anywhere in the conversation.** Long threads need message links, search, unread markers, and direct navigation.
T 133 text.html.markdown
L 11. **Reopen where the reader left off.** A saved conversation should open at the last meaningful turn. Often this is the last user message. Not the absolute bottom.
T 165 text.html.markdown
L 12. **Keep the reader’s place when layout changes.** Images load. Markdown expands. Code blocks render. Older messages appear above. None of that should make the reader lose their place.
T 188 text.html.markdown
L 13. **Handle interruptions without stealing position.** Stopping, retrying, regenerating, branching, or errors should not unexpectedly move the conversation.
T 157 text.html.markdown
L 14. **Stay responsive in long threads.** Streaming text, markdown, code, images, and long history should still feel responsive.
T 127 text.html.markdown
L 15. **Be accessible without the noise.** Keep the transcript navigable, preserve keyboard focus, and announce important events at a comfortable pace.
T 149 text.html.markdown
L 
L **Never move the reader against their intent.**
T 47 text.html.markdown
L 
L ## MessageScroller
T 18 text.html.markdown
L 
L MessageScroller is a chat transcript scroller built for these behaviors.
T 72 text.html.markdown
L `MessageScrollerProvider` owns the scroll state and transcript-row behavior:
T 76 text.html.markdown
L opening position, streamed output, new-turn anchoring, prepended history,
T 73 text.html.markdown
L visibility, and scroll controls. `MessageScroller` is the styled frame that
T 75 text.html.markdown
L renders inside it.
T 18 text.html.markdown
L 
L MessageScroller is scoped to the scroll viewport. It does not own messages, AI state,
T 85 text.html.markdown
L transport, persistence, branching, or model state. Your product code stays
T 74 text.html.markdown
L focused on composing messages, markers, tools, attachments, and prompt inputs.
T 78 text.html.markdown
L 
L It gives you the scroll behavior that chat needs, without taking over the rest
T 78 text.html.markdown
L of the chat UI. And it stays fast, even in long conversations with rich
T 71 text.html.markdown
L markdown.
