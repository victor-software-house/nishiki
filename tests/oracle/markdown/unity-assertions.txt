L ### How do the EQUAL assertions work for FLOAT and DOUBLE?

T 4 text.html.markdown markup.heading.markdown heading.3.markdown
T 58 text.html.markdown markup.heading.markdown heading.3.markdown entity.name.section.markdown
L 
L As you may know, directly checking for equality between a pair of floats or a
T 77 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L pair of doubles is sloppy at best and an outright no-no at worst. Floating point
T 80 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown

T 80 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L operations on a value. Initializing a variable to the value of 2.0 is likely to
T 79 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L result in a floating point representation of 2 x 20,but a series of
T 67 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L mathematical operations might result in a representation of 8 x 2-2
T 67 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown

T 76 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown
L equality checks to fail.
T 24 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown

T 81 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown

T 16 text.html.markdown meta.paragraph.markdown markup.inline.raw.string.markdown punctuation.definition.raw.markdown
